## 📄 Full Description
Welcome to **NewsX**, the ultimate AI-powered smart news app inspired by full-bleed vertical reels and short summaries! 

Stay informed in less than 15 seconds per story with objective, AI-summarized news. 

### 🌟 Key Features:
- ⚡ **Read in 25 Words**: Concise, short, unbiased news summaries curated by Google Gemini AI.
- 📱 **Full-Screen Vertical Reels**: Scroll smoothly like Instagram Reels or Inshorts.
- 🌐 **Multi-Lingual Support**: Instantly switch summaries between English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Marathi (मराठी).
- 🧠 **"Why It Matters" AI Insights**: Understand the key impact behind every breaking story.
- 🔥 **Personalized News Stream**: Tailored specifically to your interests (AI, Tech, Cricket, Business, Science, World).
- 🌙 **Material 3 Light & Dark Mode**: Sleek glassmorphism and modern UI designed for smooth reading.
- 💾 **Offline Reading**: Save your favorite bookmarks and read anywhere, anytime.

Download NewsX today and experience the future of news consumption!

## 🛡️ Google Play Store Data Safety Questionnaire Answers
- **Data Collected**: None shared with third parties.
- **Security**: Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS 1.3.
- **Account Deletion**: Users can request profile and data deletion within app settings.
