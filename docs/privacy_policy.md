# Privacy Policy & Terms of Service - NewsX

**Effective Date:** July 28, 2026

## 1. Introduction
NewsX ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and backend services.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Account Information**: Email, Name, and optional profile photo provided during registration.
- **Reading Preferences & History**: Selected interest categories, bookmarked articles, and reading duration used solely for AI personalisation.
- **Device Telemetry**: Basic device metrics (OS, language preference) to ensure smooth app performance.

## 3. How We Use Information
- To deliver personalized news recommendations.
- To sync bookmarks and user reading streaks across devices.
- To improve our AI summaries and news processing pipeline.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We utilize secure cloud partners:
- **Google Gemini API**: For text summarization (no user PII transmitted).
- **Firebase Authentication**: For secure OAuth sign-in.

## 5. User Rights & Data Deletion
You may edit or delete your account information anytime in the App Settings. To request complete data purge, contact `support@newsx.ai`.
